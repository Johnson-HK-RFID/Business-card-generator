## Important status notes

- reCamera Pro and TF02-Pro values are based on vendor-published specifications.
- Battery values are preliminary system targets, not the specification of a selected product.
- 4G router, PoE equipment and siren remain unselected. Their draft values are requirements only.
- Indicative runtime assumes 80% usable battery energy and must be recalculated after the loads are selected.
- The reCamera image is an official Seeed Studio product asset retained for this internal design draft. Confirm marketing usage rights before public publication.
